Everything You Need to Know on Duty-Free Car Imports in Zimbabwe

Certain individuals such as returning residents, diplomats, and government officials may qualify for duty-free vehicle importation in Zimbabwe. To apply, you must provide proof of eligibility, documentation for the vehicle, and clearance from ZIMRA. This process helps reduce costs and encourages compliant importation.

×

Why Comprehensive Insurance is Worth It for Imported Cars

Comprehensive insurance protects your vehicle against theft, fire, accidents, and natural damage. It’s especially important for imported cars due to limited parts availability and higher repair costs. Investing in coverage ensures peace of mind and financial security in case of unforeseen events.

×

How to Choose the Best Car for Your Budget in Zimbabwe

When buying a car, consider both the purchase price and long-term maintenance costs. Focus on reliable brands like Toyota, Honda, or Nissan for better resale value and easy access to parts. Always include insurance, registration, and duty in your total budget estimate.
