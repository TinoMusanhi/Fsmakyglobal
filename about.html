OUR STORY

Founded with a vision to transform the vehicle importation industry in Zimbabwe, Fsmaky Global Pvt Ltd has grown from a small startup to a trusted partner for individuals and businesses seeking reliable vehicle importation services.

Our journey began when our founders experienced firsthand the challenges and complexities of importing vehicles - the confusing paperwork, unexpected costs, and lengthy delays. We knew there had to be a better way.

Today, we've streamlined the entire process, building strong relationships with international suppliers, customs officials, and insurance providers to deliver a seamless experience for our clients.

ABOUT US

We are Fsmaky Global Pvt Ltd, a trusted vehicle importation and registration company based in Zimbabwe. Our mission is to make the process of owning a car from overseas simple, transparent, and stress free for every client.

WHAT WE DO

We assist individuals and businesses with vehicle purchasing, importation, clearance, registration, and insurance coordination. From finding the right vehicle to handling paperwork and delivery, we provide step-by-step guidance and reliable support throughout the journey.

Our comprehensive services cover every aspect of vehicle ownership, ensuring our clients can focus on enjoying their new vehicle while we handle the complexities of importation and registration.
