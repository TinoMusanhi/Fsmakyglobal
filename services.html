Our Comprehensive Services

At Fsmaky Global, we handle every step of the vehicle ownership journey with precision and care. From international sourcing to local maintenance, our end-to-end solutions ensure a seamless experience from start to finish.

Vehicle Importation

We source quality vehicles from trusted international suppliers, managing the entire import process from quotation to delivery while ensuring compliance with Zimbabwean standards.

Service Includes:

  • Verified supplier network
  • Detailed cost estimates & guidance
  • Real-time tracking until delivery
  • Pre-shipment inspections

Vehicle Clearance

Our experts navigate ZIMRA requirements and customs procedures efficiently, ensuring your vehicle clears customs quickly without unexpected delays or costs.

Service Includes:

  • Complete ZIMRA documentation
  • Accurate duty calculation support
  • Port clearance coordination
  • Logistics management

Vehicle Registration

We handle all registration requirements to get your vehicle road-legal quickly, managing paperwork and compliance so you can drive with confidence.

Service Includes:

  • Number plate registration
  • Licensing & ownership documents
  • Roadworthy compliance assistance
  • Title transfer support

Vehicle Insurance

We connect you with reputable insurance providers offering comprehensive coverage options at competitive rates, ensuring your investment stays protected.

Service Includes:

  • Third-party & comprehensive options
  • Claims assistance & renewal support
  • Partnerships with top insurers
  • Policy comparison & recommendations

Vehicle Maintenance

Our maintenance support connects you with trusted service centers and mechanics, ensuring your vehicle stays in optimal condition long after purchase.

Service Includes:

  • Verified workshop referrals
  • Maintenance scheduling reminders
  • Quality assurance checks
  • Genuine parts sourcing support
