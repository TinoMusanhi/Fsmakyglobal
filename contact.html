Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the vehicle import process take?

The import process typically takes between 4-8 weeks depending on the origin country, shipping method, and customs clearance. We'll provide you with a detailed timeline once we understand your specific requirements.

What documents do I need to import a vehicle?

You'll need a valid ID, proof of residence, and in some cases, an import license. Our team will guide you through all the necessary documentation and help you prepare everything correctly.

Do you help with vehicle registration after import?

Yes! We offer complete end-to-end service including vehicle registration, insurance, and roadworthy certification. We'll handle all the paperwork so you can focus on enjoying your new vehicle.

Can I import any type of vehicle?

Most vehicles can be imported, but there may be restrictions based on age, emissions standards, or specific regulations. Contact us with details about the vehicle you're interested in, and we'll advise you on any restrictions.
