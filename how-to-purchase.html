How to Purchase with Us

Follow our simple process to get your dream vehicle imported smoothly and securely.

Step-by-Step Guide

1

Vehicle Selection

Start by choosing your preferred vehicle from our trusted partners like Beforward or SBT Japan, or from our exclusive WhatsApp group (join here 👉 click to join).

2

Reserve Your Car

Once you’ve found your ideal car, provide your Full Name, Place of Residence, and Phone Number. Our team will confirm availability and start the booking process.

3

Receive Your Proforma Invoice

We’ll send you a Proforma Invoice with vehicle details, total cost, and payment instructions.

4

Make Your Payment

Pay conveniently via Cash at our office, Bank Transfer, or ZANACO Bank.

5

Vehicle Inspection

Your vehicle undergoes a thorough inspection in Japan or Singapore to ensure safety and quality before shipping.

6

Shipping & Transit

Vehicles are shipped to Dar es Salaam — transit usually takes about 8 weeks depending on schedules.

7

Pre-clearance & Customs

We manage pre-clearance at key points including Dar es Salaam, Tunduma, and Chirundu to fast-track your vehicle’s arrival.

8

Insurance & Plates

We assist with ZINARA or CBZ Insurance and provide temporary number plates valid for up to 14 days.

9

Nationwide Delivery

Once cleared, your vehicle is delivered anywhere in Zimbabwe — road-ready and fully documented.
