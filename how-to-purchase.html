Get in Touch With Us — How to Purchase

Step-by-Step Purchase Guide

1️⃣ Vehicle Selection

Start by choosing your preferred vehicle from our trusted partners like Beforward or SBT Japan, or from our exclusive WhatsApp group (join here 👉 [insert link]).

2️⃣ Reserve Your Car

Once you’ve found your ideal car, provide your Full Name, Place of Residence, and Phone Number. Our team will confirm availability and start the booking.

3️⃣ Receive Your Proforma Invoice

You’ll get a proforma invoice with vehicle details, price, and payment info.

4️⃣ Make Your Payment

Pay via Cash at our office, Bank Transfer, or ZANACO Bank.

5️⃣ Vehicle Inspection

Your vehicle is inspected in Japan or Singapore for safety and quality.

6️⃣ Shipping & Transit

Cars are shipped to Dar es Salaam (around 8 weeks depending on the schedule).

7️⃣ Pre-clearance & Customs

We handle pre-clearance in Dar es Salaam, Tunduma, and Chirundu.

8️⃣ Insurance & Plates

We assist with ZINARA or CBZ insurance and temporary plates valid for 14 days.

9️⃣ Nationwide Delivery

Once cleared, we deliver your car anywhere in Zimbabwe, ready for the road.
