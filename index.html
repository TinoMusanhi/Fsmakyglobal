Our Services

At Fsmaky Global Pvt Ltd, we handle every step of the vehicle ownership process with transparency, professionalism and care. Whether you're importing, clearing, or registering your vehicle, we've got you covered.

Vehicle Importation

With our partnership with major suppliers such as Beforward and SBT, and other vehicle suppliers, we provide comprehensive vehicle sourcing processes, facilitation of payments upto point of discharge.

Source Market

SBT, Beforward and China.

Vehicle Reservation

Payment Process

Shipping Process

Delivery

Vehicle Clearance

Clearance at Port of Discharge

Clearance at Transit Port

Clearance at Country of Delivery

Vehicle Registration

Get your vehicle fully licensed and road-ready without the hassle. We take care of all the registration steps to ensure compliance and peace of mind.

Clearing Documents

Invoice, Bill of Lading, Export Certificate, National ID, and Proof of Payment.

Insurance & Radio Licensing

We help you secure mandatory vehicle insurance and radio license.

VTS & Inspection

Vehicle Number Plating

Official number plate allocation and processing.

Vehicle Licensing (ZINARA)

Final ZINARA licensing to make your vehicle fully road-legal.
