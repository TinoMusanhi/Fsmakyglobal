COMPANY OVERVIEW

Fsmaky Global Pvt Ltd is a premier vehicle importation, registration, and logistics service provider dedicated to helping individuals and businesses import, clear, register, insure, and maintain vehicles with absolute ease.

We simplify the entire process from selecting a car that fits your budget to handling customs, licensing, and insurance, ensuring our clients enjoy a smooth and transparent experience from start to finish.

Our team partners with trusted suppliers, insurance companies, and clearance agents to guarantee reliability, authenticity, and competitive pricing. Whether you're buying your first car or managing a fleet, we provide step-by-step support and expert guidance every step of the way.

Our Services

At Fsmaky Global Pvt Ltd, we handle every step of the vehicle ownership process with professionalism, transparency, and care. Whether you're importing, clearing, or registering your vehicle, we've got you covered.

Vehicle Importation

We help clients source vehicles from trusted suppliers worldwide. Our team manages quotations, pre-shipment inspections, and delivery logistics ensuring every import meets Zimbabwean standards and arrives safely.

  • Supplier verification
  • Cost estimates and import guidance
  • Tracking and updates until delivery

Vehicle Clearance

We handle all customs paperwork and procedures to make sure your vehicle is cleared quickly and efficiently, navigating ZIMRA requirements with expertise.

  • ZIMRA documentation
  • Duty calculation support
  • Port clearance and logistics coordination

Vehicle Registration

Get your vehicle fully licensed and road-ready without the hassle. We take care of all the registration steps to ensure compliance and peace of mind.

  • Number plate registration
  • Licensing and ownership documents
  • Roadworthy compliance assistance

Vehicle Insurance

We connect clients with reputable insurance providers offering competitive premiums and reliable cover, ensuring your investment is protected.

  • Third-party and comprehensive cover options
  • Assistance with claims and renewals
  • Partnership with top insurance companies

Vehicle Maintenance Assistance

We help clients find trusted mechanics and service centres for regular maintenance or repairs, ensuring your vehicle stays in optimal condition.

  • Workshop referrals
  • Maintenance scheduling reminders
  • Quality assurance and parts sourcing support
